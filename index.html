§ 05 — Engagement

Four phases.
No retainers.

We diagnose, we recommend, we implement, and we hand you a system your team can run without us. The mandate has an end date by design.

Diagnose

Audit what exists. Pressure-test the brief. Define the real problem — usually not the one you arrived with.

Institutionalise

Guidelines, playbooks, and the operating system your team extends without us. We leave when the work outlives us.

§ 06 — Selected engagements

Real briefs.
Documented outcomes.

Five engagements from 2023–2024. Each starts with a diagnosis. Each ends with something that works without us in the room. Click any case to read the full diagnosis.

  1. Situation

    Three SKUs (Crispy Romaine 227g, Living Basil, Fancy range) shelf-ready for Foodland Ontario. Five vendors making decisions in parallel — packaging printer, social team, web team, retail buyer, and logistics — with no shared system.

    Decision

    Stop designing more. Build the manual first — a seven-section brand system that could be handed to any printer, any vendor, any new hire, and produce consistent output without a call to the agency.

    Implementation

    Identity (V-mark + wordmark). Brand manual v2.0. Pack system across three retail SKUs. Foodland Ontario and Non-GMO Project compliance built into every file. Retail collateral. Website. 392 Enterprise Drive, Welland, ON — on shelf.

    "Three printers, two social vendors, and a web team — all working from the same v2.0 spec with zero agency calls in between."

  2. Situation

    Shapewear, activewear, and body scrub each shot in a different visual environment. No brand line. Celebrity campaigns with Santoshi Shetty and Neha Kakkar needed before year-end. 900+ creators to brief with no single system.

    Decision

    Reframe from "shapewear" to wearable engineering — body-first design that adapts to you. One camera language across all three categories. Build the creator brief system before going to celebrities, not after.

    Implementation

    Brand line + creative direction. 50+ SKU shoot: soft architectural shadow for shapewear, studio-grey for activewear, lifestyle-black for the body range. Two celebrity drops. 18-post social content system handed to the in-house team.

    "Two celebrity campaigns shipped. The social system now runs without us on retainer — 900+ creators briefed from one document."

  3. Situation

    Seven SKUs production-ready. No visual identity. No SKU copy. No Instagram. A launch date approaching and a category that rewards storytelling — shelf presence earned bottle by bottle, not brand by brand.

    Decision

    The font choice is the flavour cue. Each SKU gets a shared silhouette and Italian tricolour cap, but its own typographic personality — Aranciata in citrus orange script, Limonata in chalked yellow, Pompelmo Fragola in confident pink. SKU copy written as stories, not ingredient lists.

    Implementation

    Full identity + seven-SKU range system. Aranciata · Gassosa · Limonata · Pompelmo Fragola · Spuma Bionda · Spumotto · Tonica Lemongrass — each with a written origin story. 90-day Instagram content engine. Catalog and in-market collateral.

    "Share in our memories of a drink our Grandmothers made squeezing fresh lemons on a hot summer day, with our Limonata." — SKU copy, written for launch

  4. Situation

    Multiple marks across app screens, partner decks, and launch collateral. No clear-space rules. No colour specifications. New hires and new vendors both guessing. The app was live — the brand wasn't.

    Decision

    Credibility is drawn, not claimed. Build an eight-section guideline from mark construction through colour and typography — so every new screen, every new swag piece, every new partner deck starts from one source of truth.

    Implementation

    Leaf-arrow mark (motion + trust in one shape). Clear-space system. Three colour variations on dark/light/brand-green (#2C6A3F). Nexa Heavy + Montserrat type pair. Icon family. Applications: app UI, cup, notebook, apparel.

    "Eight-page brand guideline shipped. One source of truth for the app team, the print vendors, and the merch runs — whoever picks it up next, ships consistent."

  5. Situation

    Strong portfolio of real Toronto projects — envelope, roofing, interior fit-outs, MEP, electrical. All undocumented. Enterprise buyers being pitched verbally, with no proof they could open on a screen or send ahead of a meeting.

    Decision

    Construction sells with proof, not promises. Build the site around the gallery — real photography from real Toronto sites. The project you land on is the one the procurement lead recognises. Ship fast enough to use in the first enterprise pitch.

    Implementation

    Website strategy, copywriting, and end-to-end build. Photography direction on live Toronto construction sites. Project gallery across four phases: envelope + roofing, interior finishes, MEP, electrical infrastructure.

    "Live in 21 days. Used in the first post-launch enterprise pitch, on a board-room screen, before the dev environment was archived."
